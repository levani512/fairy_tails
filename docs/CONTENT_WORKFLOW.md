
## Audio libraries and direct uploads

Story covers, thumbnails, illustrations, narration, and character images use Browse to upload directly. Selected files show Remove; remove the selection to browse for a different file. Remove only clears the field and does not delete the media record. The edit icon remains available to adjust media metadata such as Alt.

Under Audio Library in the admin menu, Background Music and Sound Effects each have named entries with required audio uploads. Add entries there, then use Choose from library in story/page Background Music or page Sound Effects. The picker searches names, paginates results, previews audio, and assigns the existing media file without duplicating it. Pending audio cannot be selected until processing completes. Direct Browse uploads are also available in these fields; they are not automatically added as named library entries.

Existing story audio assignments are preserved. Choosing a library item stores its media reference; replacing the library entry's audio later does not silently replace audio already selected by stories.

## Cover and ending pages

Cover Page appears before the numbered pages, and The End Page appears after them. Each has an image/video illustration, optional voice-over upload, delay, Background Music, Sound Effects, and additional language overrides. They have no narration text or word synchronization. If no illustration is selected, playback uses the story's localized cover image. A language without a voice-over remains silent.

Starting a story opens its cover with a forward arrow. After the numbered pages, the ending shows a Home button that completes the story and returns to the lobby. Continue Reading still resumes saved progress. Numbered story pages retain their text and timing requirements.

## Story quizzes

Open the collapsed Story Quiz section after The End Page. Add questions and 2–6 answer choices for each question. Correct answer uses radio buttons: the first answer is selected automatically, choosing another clears the previous selection, and deleting the selected answer selects the first remaining one. An optional explanation appears after checking an answer. Save the story to make its quiz available; questions and answers can be reordered with the array handles. The same quiz text is currently used for every story language.

Take a test on the ending opens the quiz in the illustration area and stops story audio. Questions appear above their answers, with Exit and Next on the right. Children select one answer and press Next: the selected answer turns green or red, a correct/wrong sound plays (unless Sound Effects is off), and the quiz advances automatically after 1.5 seconds. Optional explanations appear during feedback. Results show the score and Try again. Exit returns directly to the lobby, including during feedback. Retry resets the score; leaving the quiz discards the current attempt. Scores are not stored. Portrait phones retain the rotate prompt. Stories without questions show a disabled test button. Quiz content is included in the story manifest, including newly downloaded offline copies.

Leave a quiz question's Answers list empty to make it open-ended. Its question is centered over the illustration, and Next advances immediately without an answer, scoring, or correct/wrong feedback. Multiple-choice questions still require 2–6 answers and exactly one correct choice. Results count only multiple-choice questions; an entirely open-ended quiz shows a completion message without a score.
